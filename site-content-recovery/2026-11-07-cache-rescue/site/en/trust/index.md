<p>This page describes our working principles. It is not a certificate wall. Where we are still maturing, we say so.</p>
<h2>Security posture</h2>
<p>Our systems are built with least-privilege access, secret management, encrypted communication and auditability by default.</p>
<h2>Governance</h2>
<p>Architecture decisions, ownership and standards are recorded and versioned. Governance is not a separate function; it is how we build.</p>
<h2>Responsible AI</h2>
<p>We treat AI as infrastructure that must be observable, testable and overrideable. Models are tools; decisions remain accountable.</p>
<h2>Observability</h2>
<p>If it cannot be observed, it cannot be operated. We instrument for metrics, logs, traces and structured events from the start.</p>
<h2>Open source</h2>
<p>RBX products are developed in public repositories. Issues, discussions and contributions are welcome where appropriate.</p>
<h2>Reliability</h2>
<p>We design for failure modes, degraded operation and recovery. Uptime is a function of discipline, not optimism.</p>
