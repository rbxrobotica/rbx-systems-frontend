<h2>Working at RBX</h2>
<p>RBX is a small, technical and remote-first company. The work combines product engineering, infrastructure, applied research and operations. People who design a solution also take part in validation, documentation and the learning that follows a production release.</p>
<p>Autonomy here means clear accountability. Important decisions need evidence, explicit trade-offs and a recovery path. We do not confuse speed with haste or complexity with quality.</p>
<h2>Profiles that fit the team</h2>
<p>We are interested in people who work well in one or more of these areas:</p>
<ul>
<li>Software engineering, backend and distributed systems.</li>
<li>Applied AI, model evaluation, RAG, agents and LLMOps.</li>
<li>DevOps, platform engineering, Kubernetes and observability.</li>
<li>Product, UX/UI and research for complex operational tools.</li>
<li>Operations, client relationships and technical business development.</li>
</ul>
<p>Experience helps, but the ability to learn, write clearly and take responsibility matters as much as a list of technologies.</p>
<h2>How we evaluate</h2>
<p>The process depends on the role, but it seeks to answer simple questions: do you understand the problem, can you make your decisions legible, and can you deliver something another person can operate? We prefer focused conversations and work samples over artificial stages.</p>
<h2>Open applications</h2>
<p>We do not always maintain a public list of open positions. We still welcome introductions from people who identify with RBX&#39;s work. Send a short note with your area of interest, relevant links and resume through the <a href="/contact">contact page</a>.</p>
