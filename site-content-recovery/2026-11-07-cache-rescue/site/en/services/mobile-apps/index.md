<h2>What we deliver</h2>
<p>RBX develops Android and iOS applications connected to APIs, identity, payments, notifications and operational data. We choose a native or cross-platform approach based on performance, device access and maintenance requirements.</p>
<ul>
<li>Product discovery and prototypes that validate critical flows.</li>
<li>Application, backend and API contracts treated as one system.</li>
<li>Authentication, secure storage and sensitive-data protection.</li>
<li>Synchronization, caching and offline operation when continuity matters.</li>
<li>Metrics, logs, crash reporting and release monitoring.</li>
</ul>
<h2>When to hire us</h2>
<p>The service fits digital products with recurring use, field teams, customer support, logistics, data collection or experiences that need device capabilities. We also modernize unstable applications or codebases that are difficult to release.</p>
<h2>How we work</h2>
<p>We map journeys, network conditions, permissions and synchronization risks before implementation. Every important mutation gets visible state, duplicate-submit protection and failure handling. Releases go through tests, permission review and a rollback plan compatible with app stores.</p>
<p>When the application depends on specialized integrations or data, we combine the project with <a href="/services/api-integration">API integration</a>, <a href="/services/custom-systems">custom systems</a> and <a href="/services/cloud-solutions">cloud solutions</a>.</p>
