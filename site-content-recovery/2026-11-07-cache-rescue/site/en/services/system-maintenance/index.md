<h2>What we deliver</h2>
<p>RBX takes over existing systems or continues operating what it builds. The service combines incident response, preventive maintenance and planned evolution, with priorities driven by operational impact.</p>
<ul>
<li>Initial assessment of code, infrastructure, dependencies and risks.</li>
<li>Defect correction and root-cause investigation.</li>
<li>Runtime, library, database and pipeline upgrades.</li>
<li>Observability, alerts, backups and recovery tests.</li>
<li>Prioritized technical backlog and updated documentation.</li>
</ul>
<h2>When to hire us</h2>
<p>This service fits systems that depend on a few people, carry vulnerable versions, suffer repeated incidents or lack a safe deployment routine. It also helps teams restore predictability before adding features.</p>
<h2>How we work</h2>
<p>The transition starts with read-only access, an inventory and severity criteria. Changes move through version control, review and a reproducible pipeline. Incidents produce evidence, a correction and a preventive action, not only an emergency patch.</p>
<p>We define scope and service level explicitly. Maintenance does not mean unlimited availability or a promise of no failures. It means making risk visible, reducing recurrence and maintaining a tested recovery path. When the foundation needs to change, we connect the work to <a href="/services/technical-consulting">technical consulting</a> and <a href="/services/cloud-solutions">cloud solutions</a>.</p>
