Starting today, the [RBX Journal](/journal) can be followed via RSS, free. The official feed lives at:

- `https://rbxsystems.ch/rss.xml` for the articles in English
- `https://rbx.ia.br/rss.xml` for the articles in Portuguese

Paste either address into your favorite RSS reader and you are done: every new article reaches you as soon as it is published. If your reader supports autodiscovery, pointing it at any page of the site is enough to find the feed.

## Why RSS in 2026

Because it is the most honest way to distribute technical content. RSS has no algorithm deciding what you see, does not ask for your email, does not track your reading, and does not depend on any platform sitting between us and you. Your reader fetches the feed when you want, and you read at your own pace. For a Journal that writes about governance, evidence and infrastructure sovereignty, any other channel would be inconsistent with its own content.

There is also an engineering detail we enjoy: the feed is served by the same sovereign content layer that powers the whole site. When we publish an article, it shows up in the feed in about a minute, with no rebuild and no deploy, through the same mechanism we describe in [our engineering articles](/journal).

## What you will receive

The Journal publishes field notes from real systems in production: what we learned operating [governed agentic autonomy](/blog/2026-08-01-governed-autonomy-distributed-systems), what a 17 GB event log taught us about [audit versus telemetry](/blog/2026-08-01-auditoria-ou-telemetria), and what turns red when [a pipeline dies silently](/blog/2026-08-02-o-painel-dizia-synced). Engineering as it actually happens, mistakes included.

Subscribe, read on your own time, and if an article sparks a conversation, [talk to RBX](/contato).
